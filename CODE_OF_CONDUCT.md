# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
CloudDown.Editor community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that harasses, demeans, or excludes others is not tolerated.

## Adoption

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version
2.1**. The full text — including the detailed standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope,
and enforcement guidelines — is available at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

By participating, you agree to uphold that Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to
the project maintainer at **32693609+digitalninjae@users.noreply.github.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its
community.
